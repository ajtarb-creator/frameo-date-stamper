Your photos. Their original dates. Ready for Frameo.

Step 1 · Select your photos

If the photo picker returns without showing photos, try the visible Choose Files control and select Browse or Photo Library. If HEIC images fail, try JPEG images first.

Everything is processed on your device. Original pictures are never changed. Photos without an original camera date stay unstamped unless you choose a suggested date or enter one yourself. You can use a full date, month and year, or year only.

Return to the share sheet, choose Frameo, and follow its prompts to select your frame.

Batch sharing: Apple Photos can share several photos with Frameo. Safari may offer a different kind of multi-file share sheet, where Frameo is absent. If that happens, use Try sharing batch → Save Images, then select those images in Apple Photos and share them together to Frameo.

Shared album tip: If a photo won't load from a shared album, open it once in Apple Photos, then return and select it here.
